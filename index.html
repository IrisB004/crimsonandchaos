Crimson & Chaos is a complete 78-card tarot deck built on the traditional Rider–Waite–Smith system and rendered in trash polka style — the raw, collision-course art style born in German tattoo studios.

Photorealism crashes into graphic abstraction. Only three colors survive: black, bone-white, and a red that does all the talking.

Trash polka is controlled destruction — hyper-real imagery fused with bold red brushstrokes, dripping ink, geometric shrapnel, and splatter that feels less like decoration and more like evidence.

In Crimson & Chaos, that becomes a visual language all its own.
